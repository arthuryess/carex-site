Need help or found a problem?

Open an issue on GitHub and describe what happened, what you expected, and your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.

Common questions

Why don't I see fuel economy yet?

Economy is worked out between full fill-ups. Log at least two full fill-ups with the fuel amount and the odometer reading.

I typed the wrong odometer reading. How do I fix it?

Open the expense and correct the reading. The vehicle's current mileage is worked out from its readings, so it updates straight away.

Why am I not getting reminders?

Check that notifications for Carex are allowed in the iOS Settings app, and that the vehicle has its insurance or registration dates filled in.

How do I get my data out?

Use Settings › Export Data to create a CSV file you can open in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your device. See the privacy policy.
